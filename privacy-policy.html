1. Introduction

BeefStick ("we," "our," or "us") operates the BeefStick mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. By using BeefStick, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

Account Information

  • Email address — Used for account creation, authentication, and communication

Health & Fitness Data

  • Body measurements — Current weight, target weight, height, and date of birth
  • Gender — Used to calculate personalized nutrition targets
  • Fitness goals — Goal timeframe and weight targets
  • Workout data — Exercises performed, sets, and workout history
  • Nutrition data — Meal entries including meal name, type, protein grams, and calories

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information — Device type and operating system version, used for app compatibility
  • Usage data — App interaction data used to improve the user experience

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Calculate personalized protein and calorie targets based on your body metrics and goals
  • Generate customized workout plans
  • Track your workout history and nutrition intake
  • Display your progress, streaks, and weekly summaries
  • Process and manage your subscription
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve and optimize the App

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is protected using industry-standard security measures:

  • Cloud Database — All user data is stored securely in Supabase, which uses PostgreSQL with encryption at rest and in transit
  • Authentication — Account authentication is handled through Supabase Auth with secure token-based sessions
  • Row-Level Security — Database access is restricted so users can only access their own data
  • HTTPS — All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services:

  • Supabase — Cloud database, authentication, and backend services (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Superwall — Subscription paywall management (Superwall Privacy Policy)
  • Apple — In-app purchase and subscription billing through the App Store (Apple Privacy Policy)

These services may collect information as described in their respective privacy policies. We do not sell your personal data to any third parties.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers — With third-party services listed above, solely to operate the App
  • Legal Requirements — If required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • Safety — To protect the rights, property, or safety of BeefStick, our users, or the public

7. Your Rights and Controls

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in the App
  • Update your profile information within the App
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export a copy of your data upon request
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

8. Data Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at Beefstick.co@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." Include the email address associated with your account.

Upon receiving your request, we will:

  1. Verify your identity
  2. Delete all personal data associated with your account, including profile information, workout history, and meal entries
  3. Confirm deletion via email

Account deletion will be processed within 30 days of a verified request. Note that canceling your subscription through Apple does not delete your account data.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. If you request account deletion, your data will be permanently removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

10. Children's Privacy

BeefStick is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete such information from our servers.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at Beefstick.co@gmail.com so we can take appropriate action.

11. International Users

BeefStick is operated from the United States. If you are accessing the App from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

12. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

We comply with applicable data protection regulations:

  • GDPR (EU Users) — You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, and port your data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.
  • CCPA (California Users) — You have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.
