Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Go to the login screen and tap "Forgot Password." Enter the email address associated with your account, and we'll send you a link to reset your password.

How do I cancel my subscription?

BeefStick subscriptions are managed through Apple. To cancel:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Find BeefStick in your subscription list
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

After canceling, you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period. No partial refunds are issued.

How do I request a refund?

Since all payments are processed through Apple, refund requests must go through Apple directly. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find your BeefStick purchase, and submit a refund request.

How are my nutrition targets calculated?

BeefStick calculates your personalized protein and calorie targets using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which factors in your weight, height, age, and gender. Your goal timeframe and target weight are also used to adjust your daily calorie target.

How do workouts get generated?

When you select muscle groups and tap "Start Workout," BeefStick generates a workout plan with 4 sets (5 if all muscle groups are selected). Each set contains 3 exercises from the same muscle group, including a mix of primary (compound) and secondary (isolation) movements. You can substitute any exercise to get a different one.

What do the green and red dots on the meals calendar mean?

The calendar dots indicate whether you met both your protein and calorie goals for that day. A green dot means both goals were met. A red dot means one or both goals were not reached.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use Supabase for secure cloud storage with row-level security, meaning only you can access your own data. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account and all associated data, send an email to Beefstick.co@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include the email associated with your account. Deletion will be processed within 30 days.
